# Privileged Access Requests

Northstar uses separate privileged access for administrative work on production systems, container platforms, monitoring systems, and shared infrastructure.

Standard employee accounts must not be granted direct administrator privileges.

## Request process

Privileged access requests are submitted through the Access Management Portal. The request must include:
- the target system;
- the business reason;
- the required role;
- the requested duration.

Manager approval is required. Production access also requires approval from the system owner.

Privileged access is normally time-limited and reviewed every 90 days.

## Passwords and credentials

Privileged credentials are separate from normal employee passwords. A reset of the corporate employee password does not automatically reset privileged local credentials on systems that do not use single sign-on.

## Emergency access

During a Severity 1 incident, the incident commander may authorize emergency access. Emergency access must be reviewed after the incident.
