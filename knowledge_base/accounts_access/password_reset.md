# Corporate Password Reset

Northstar employees use a single corporate identity for email, VPN, internal applications, and most developer services.

## Self-service reset

Employees who remember their current password should change it through the Identity Portal under **Security > Change Password**.

Employees who have forgotten their password should use **Forgot Password** on the Identity Portal sign-in page. Identity verification requires the employee's registered MFA method.

After a successful reset, the new password may take up to five minutes to work across all connected services.

## When self-service reset fails

Contact the Service Desk when:
- the registered MFA device is unavailable;
- the account is locked;
- the Identity Portal cannot verify the employee;
- the new password still fails after five minutes.

The Service Desk may verify the employee's identity and issue a temporary recovery code. Employees must never ask another employee to reset a password on their behalf.

## VPN note

The corporate VPN uses the same corporate identity. After changing a password, employees may need to disconnect and reconnect the VPN before the new credentials are accepted.
