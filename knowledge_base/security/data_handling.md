# Internal Data Handling

Northstar information is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted.

## Approved storage

Internal business data must be stored only in approved company-managed systems.

Confidential and Restricted information must not be uploaded to personal cloud storage, personal email, or unapproved AI services.

## Engineering systems

Logs, configuration files, model artifacts, and datasets may contain sensitive information even when they appear technical.

Employees should use approved repositories and storage services for engineering data.

## Incidents

Suspected exposure of Confidential or Restricted information must be reported through the security incident process.
